Jarvis WorkOS · Omarchy

The desktop that talks back

Real screenshots from this project's test runs: Jarvis inside the Caelestia shell, the island at the top of the screen, HUD cards on the Omarchy desktop, and the Living Workspace.

./install-jarvis.sh --shell caelestia && jarvis shell caelestia

Caelestia + Jarvis

--shell caelestia

Caelestia takes the bar (left), the frame, dashboard, notifications and lock screen. Jarvis hangs from the top border as one of its drawers, and its colours come from your wallpaper.

The island

Super+Alt+I

The same island runs in Omarchy's shell and in Caelestia. It rests as a thin pill, peeks on activity and opens into a card.

On the Omarchy desktop

ui.hud = true

With Omarchy's own shell, Jarvis adds floating HUD cards at the screen edge: globes, gauges, documents, the command bar.

Living Workspace and documents

Super+Alt+W · --genoffice

Results Jarvis can show and edit: comparisons, files, generated mini-apps, real Office documents and decks.

Where these come from. Every image is from the repository's evidence/ folder: the headless test desktop, the VM checks and the island preview. On your machine the wallpaper, colours and content will be your own.
